PlateCount

Count bacterial colonies from a photo of an agar plate

  1. 1Photograph the plate
  2. 2The AI agent counts and checks itself
  3. 3Get count + CFU/mL with flags

Use a power of ten: 10^-2, 1:100 or 0.01. Left blank: undiluted, 1 mL.

Your photo is sent to Meta's API to count colonies and is deleted within 24 hours.
